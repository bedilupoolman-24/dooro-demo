# Dooro demo

Clickable demo of Dooro, a home-discovery app: a vertical feed of homes, a real map with a
freehand "draw your area" tool, home details, saved homes and a profile.

Discover has three feeds you swipe between: Following (posts and new homes from accounts you follow,
with agent stories on top), For You (listings in your search) and Explore (homes from around the
world, just for fun). You can follow agents, creators and neighbourhoods, and save homes and posts
to boards that you share with one link. Creators, posts and agents are fictional.

All homes are fictional (placed on real Amsterdam streets) and the pictures are illustrations.
No backend: everything runs in the browser and saves to the device.

Install on a phone: open the site in Safari (iPhone) or Chrome (Android) → Share / menu → Add to Home Screen.
